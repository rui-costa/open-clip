### Users
Content creators and social media managers who manage long-form video content and need to efficiently generate, edit, and upload viral clips to social platforms.

### Brand Personality
Efficient, bold, fast-paced. 

### Aesthetic Direction
Bold & Brutalist. High-contrast, large-scale typography, sharp geometric borders, vibrant accent colors, and a raw, utilitarian feel. The interface should feel like a high-performance tool for rapid content production.

### Design Principles
1. **Utility-First**: Every element must support the primary job of managing and publishing clips.
2. **Bold Visual Hierarchy**: Use scale and contrast to instantly communicate status (e.g., pending vs done) and call attention to actionable tasks.
3. **Intentional Brutalism**: Use harsh lines, sharp corners, and high-impact color (accent) to create a distinctive, tool-like aesthetic that feels fast and precise.
4. **Action-Oriented**: Interactions (edit, save, upload) should be immediately accessible and have clear visual feedback.
